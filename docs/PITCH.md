## Elevator pitch (3 sentences — for the summary field)
Remesa is a Telegram AI agent that sends USDC home to Latin America in seconds,
turning "send $5 to mama at 0x…" into a confirmed on-chain transfer on Base.
What makes it different: the agent pays for its own tools — a $0.01 FX quote and
a $0.05 sanctions screen — with real x402 micropayments, then hands back an
itemized receipt where every fee is an auditable on-chain transaction. The result
is a flat $0.06 fee versus Western Union's ~$9.90 on a $200 remittance — agentic
commerce made concrete, built on Coinbase AgentKit, x402, Circle USDC, and Base.
